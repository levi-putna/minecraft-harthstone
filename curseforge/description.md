# Anchorstone

**Channel for 5 seconds and return home.** Anchorstone adds a reusable magic stone to Minecraft Bedrock Edition that teleports you back to your bed or respawn anchor, from anywhere, even the Nether or the End.

## How it works

Hold the Anchorstone and use it, then stand still while the power builds:

- A ring of blue flames spins and rises around you.
- After 2 seconds you start to float off the ground.
- The world wobbles, a portal hum builds, and the screen fades to deep blue.
- You arrive home in a burst of blue flames.

Take any damage or walk away and the spell is broken. All the effects stop at once, and you can try again straight away.

## Features

- **Return home from anywhere:** your bed or respawn anchor, across dimensions. No bed? You land near world spawn, just like a normal respawn.
- **A real channel:** 5 seconds with a progress bar. Damage or walking interrupts it, so you can't use it to escape a fight.
- **Smooth arrival:** your destination loads while you channel, so you land on solid ground, not in the void.
- **Free starter stone:** every player gets one the first time they join, in new or existing worlds.
- **Reusable and craftable:** the stone is never used up, and you can craft more.

## Crafting

Shapeless recipe at a crafting table:

| Ingredient | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Redstone dust | 1 |
| Cobblestone | 1 |
| Lapis lazuli ore (or deepslate lapis ore) | 1 |

Mining lapis ore normally drops lapis lazuli. To collect the ore block itself, use a pickaxe with **Silk Touch**.

## Installation

1. Download the `.mcaddon` file.
2. Double-click it. Minecraft opens and imports two packs: *Anchorstone* and *Anchorstone Resources*.
3. Edit your world, go to **Behavior Packs**, and activate **Anchorstone**. The resource pack turns on with it.
4. Load the world. You'll be given your Anchorstone.

**Requires Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.21.90 or newer.** Works on Windows, Xbox, mobile and Bedrock Dedicated Server. No experimental toggles needed.

## Upgrading from Hearthstone

Anchorstone 2.0.0 was previously called Hearthstone. Old Hearthstone items disappear when you update, and every player gets a free Anchorstone the next time they join.
